    How this check works

    When enabled, your browser analyzes live hand landmarks and face blendshapes on this device. It can show whether your hands and face are in frame, and whether hand or facial movement was detected. Video is not recorded or uploaded. These cues do not confirm a sign’s meaning, facial grammar, or correctness.

    🔒 Camera permission is optional. You can still use lesson prompts and track practice without turning it on.
